Our Story

Bread Is Patience, Baked

Bread Board started with one oven, one flour sack, and a stubborn belief that good bread can't be rushed. Every loaf we sell still follows that rule.

From One Oven to a Neighborhood Favorite

What began as early mornings in a small kitchen has grown into a bakery trusted by our whole community. We still measure success the same way we did on day one: by the crust, the crumb, and whether you come back for more.

No shortcuts, no additives, no rushing the ferment. Just flour, water, salt, and time — the same four ingredients bakers have trusted for centuries.
